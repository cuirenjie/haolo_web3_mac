# Elliott Wave runtime rules

- Standard motive: wave 2 must not pass the origin; wave 3 must pass wave 1 and cannot be the shortest motive wave; wave 4 must not fully retrace wave 3 or enter wave 1 territory; wave 5 must progress unless a small, internally verified truncation exists; internal evidence must support 5-3-5-3-5.
- A leading diagonal is valid only in wave 1 or A. An ending diagonal is valid only in wave 5 or C. Position, wedge, retracement, shortest-wave, and internal evidence are all required.
- Zigzag requires 5-3-5. Flat variants require 3-3-5. Double Three requires verified W, X, and Y corrections.
- An arbitrary odd lower-degree pivot count is not subdivision proof. Filter sub-0.3 ATR noise first; a longer raw sequence may be reduced only to a first/last anchored 3- or 5-wave skeleton that independently passes the corresponding hard geometry rules. A 7-wave complex correction or 9-wave motive extension must pass its explicit grouped structure; otherwise reject it.
- Preserve stable candidate and point IDs. Keep main and alternate counts, but draw only the primary path.
- Map analysis, structure, and execution degrees deterministically. The default 4H mapping is 1H structure plus 15m execution, using only candles closed by the locked snapshot time.
- A child candidate must project in the same direction as the selected parent candidate and fit the active parent segment's time/price envelope. Counter-trend and out-of-envelope children are observation evidence only.
- Tactical triggers prefer a future execution-degree confirmation or confirmed micro swing plus a 0.15 ATR buffer. Preferred distance is at most 1.5 execution ATR and 2 ATR remains the normal prompt range; if lower-degree evidence is unavailable, use the nearest confirmed structure, wave invalidation, or volatility anchor and label the conditional plan as a fallback. Evidence expires after 12 execution bars.
- A tactical stop comes from an unbroken, confirmed support zone for longs or resistance zone for shorts whenever available. Build the zone from confirmed pivots across analysis/structure/execution degrees, cluster repeated/confluent touches without transitive over-expansion, then place the stop beyond the outer zone boundary with at least 0.15 ATR (and 2 ticks / 10% zone-width where larger) buffer. For a 4H/1H analysis, prefer the nearest analysis/structure-degree zone around current price before considering an execution pivot; this keeps a short stop near the higher-timeframe resistance instead of a fragile 15m level. If the higher-degree zone is unavailable, the nearest confirmed pivot or wave invalidation may be used as an explicitly labelled fallback; an ATR volatility anchor is the final fallback when a candidate and ATR exist. Stop distance must be at least 0.6 execution ATR, preferred within 0.8–2 ATR. A higher-degree structural stop may extend to 6% of the trigger when its zone remains within 5% of current price; execution-only stops retain the 3.5 ATR / 3% envelope. Expand a too-tight stop outward and warn on an exceptional distance instead of deleting an otherwise valid conditional plan. Macro invalidation remains a scenario-cancellation boundary. Targets must retain their measurement basis: completed motive retracement uses 0.382/0.5/0.618, completed correction retracement uses 0.382/0.618/1.0, and a completed cycle may project 0.382/0.618/1.0 of its prior impulse from the correction terminal. Candidate structural projections may be added only on the favorable side of the trigger; if measured targets are too short, append clearly labelled risk-multiple fallback targets.
- Use the user's explicit minimum estimated net risk/reward for every executable target, including the first; when the user has not set one, default to 1:0.4. Whenever the measured final target leaves enough directional room, prefer a three-exit ladder with T1 between 1:0.4 and 1:0.6 and T2 between 1:0.8 and 1:1, using the measured final target as T3. Preserve the measured final ratio when it is outside 1:1.3–1:1.5, while still keeping any executable preferred earlier exits. Preserve measured targets that meet the applicable floor; if price space or the user's explicit minimum prevents a complete ladder, keep the current measured ratios so an otherwise executable setup is not discarded. Use labelled risk-multiple fallbacks when needed. If current price, direction, or ATR is available, retain a single-side conditional execution plan even when a preferred gate is not met and expose the gate as a warning; only missing essential market facts may return no executable trigger.
- Preserve the host's historical-range boundary during auto-expansion; never add candles after a requested historical endpoint.
- Keep `ai/wave` and its five semantic color tokens isolated.
- Return `insufficient_data` rather than packaging an invalid motive as a complete cycle. A motive whose price hard rules pass but whose lower-degree 5-3-5-3-5 evidence is not yet visible may remain a `hard_price_rules_pending_lower_degree_structure` tentative single impulse for chart continuity; do not mark it confirmed or combine it with ABC/WXY until the child evidence arrives.
